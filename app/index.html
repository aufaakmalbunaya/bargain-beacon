How we check a deal

  1. Find it in a source that names the store. A deal comes from the store's own Black Friday release or a deals roundup that names the store and the price.
  2. Compare against the usual price. The discount on each card is worked out from the sale price and the usual price, so the maths always adds up.
  3. Say whether it is really a deal. 55% off or more is a true low, 40–54% a strong deal, 25–39% a solid deal, under 25% a modest cut. Small cuts are labelled small.
  4. Show the check time and the source. Every deal says when its price was read and where, and links out to the store. Prices move fast on Black Friday — check the live price before you buy.
